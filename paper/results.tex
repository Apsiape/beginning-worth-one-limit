\section{The main results}\label{sec:results}
The following statements give the comparison before the constructions.
Proofs occupy Sections~\ref{sec:consensus}--\ref{sec:selection}.
The two substantial realization inputs are the noiseless
self-organization of Hellouin de Menibus--Sablik and the reliable
stacking of Toom and G\'acs--Reif, in the forms stated below.

\begin{theorem}[Consensus and begun ceilings]\label{thm:ceilings}
For computable $K$, an eternal depth-$d$ law constant on a nonempty
effective $\Sigma^0_2$ class of stationary TI states belongs to
$\D_{d+1}$. At depth zero its common value is computable.
For bounded linear families, agreement and existence of every
intermediate limit on all extreme states suffice for agreement
on all stationary TI states. For computable $\lambda$ and $K$,
a begun depth-$d$ law belongs to $\D_{d+2}$.
These ceilings transfer to common robust values on open kernel sets.
They also hold for finite stationary-history statistics, counting
each additional limiting lag as one limit.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Realization without a distinguished origin]
\label{thm:realization}
For each $\beta\in\D_3\cap[0,1]$ there is a TI positive-rate PCA
on $\Z^3$ and a nonempty open kernel region $U_\beta$ with the
following property. From the canonical computable cloned Bernoulli
seed, a fixed uniform gap-readout schema has begun scale-depth-one
law $\beta$ for every $K\in U_\beta$. Its initial version uses
the world's age for copy spacing. The same value holds for the clone of the blocked image of
every full-support, one-cell-shift-invariant ergodic row law on
the pre-blocking alphabet $A$. This quantifier concerns $A$-row
laws before blocking, rather than arbitrary shift-ergodic laws
on the physical alphabet $\Sigma=A^B$. The uniform Bernoulli
row on $\Sigma$ is included. The program is in the finite rule, not at a site.
At every finite scale depth $d\geq0$, a corresponding uniform
schema attains exactly $\D_{d+2}\cap[0,1]$ over computable begun
constructions of this type. The time limit is innermost.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Clock-free realization and selected late states]
\label{thm:clockfree}
The depth-one realization can be chosen with a statistic depending
on scale only and with every required inner time limit existing.
It has value $\beta$ at every kernel in its open region. The same
clock-free depth-one law has value $\beta$ at every ordinary and
every Ces\`aro cluster state of the begun orbit. At computable $K$,
the Ces\`aro cluster class is a nonempty $\Pi^0_2$ class of
stationary TI states. If $\beta\notin\D_2$, none of its members
has a low name, and no nonempty effective $\Sigma^0_2$ stationary
class has this consensus value at depth one.
\end{theorem}
Clock removal here uses dyadic renewal parameters and a continuous
plateau quantizer. A uniform small error for an unmodified ratio
would not establish its inner time limit.

\begin{theorem}[Fluctuation transfer]\label{thm:transfer}
Let $K$ have positive rates, and let a begun depth-$d$ law from
a computable seed exist. There is an effective transformation of
its seed, statistic family and cone geometry into an eternal
depth-$(d+1)$ space-time statistic having exactly that value in
every stationary state of the same PCA, whether TI or not.
It preserves robustness on any open set on which the begun law
is robust. It does not query the target real. The added index
is a time lag in the eternal path law.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Sharp arithmetic comparison]\label{cor:sharp}
Among laws with values in $[0,1]$, at every $d\geq0$, robust
all-state eternal laws of depth $d+1$
and the begun constructions of depth $d$ have exact image
$\D_{d+2}\cap[0,1]$ when dynamics and admissible readouts are
varied as specified above. In particular every $\D_3$ value has
a robust all-state eternal realization at depth two. A value
outside $\D_{d+1}$ cannot have eternal consensus at depth $d$
on a nonempty effective $\Sigma^0_2$ stationary class at computable
parameters. Transfer supplies one sufficient extra limit; it
does not prove a minimal cost of one extra limit for each
individual law. A computable constant already has eternal depth zero.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Static formulation]\label{cor:gibbs}
For the normalized PCA interaction $\varphi_K=-\log K$ on
$\Z^3\times\Z$, the depth-two fluctuation law is a two-scale
spatial law with value $\beta$ in every fully translation-invariant
Gibbs state. The lag is the fourth-direction slab separation.
Robustness is relative to this PCA interaction family, not to
arbitrary four-dimensional interaction perturbations.
\end{corollary}

For fixed targets outside $\D_2$, the begun values at computable
data cannot be learned with positive probability by the effective
finite-history inquiries described in Section~\ref{subsec:reach}.
This is a fixed-target assertion, not a restriction on every
realized random limit. The selected stationary class in
Theorem~\ref{thm:clockfree} is not an effective closed consensus
class. There is consequently no conflict with
Theorem~\ref{thm:ceilings}, nor with computability of a unique
stationary PCA measure.
