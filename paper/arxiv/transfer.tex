\section{Exact fluctuation transfer}\label{sec:transfer}
\subsection{Finite patterns and their future cones}
\begin{lemma}[Positive patterns and the conditional cone identity]
\label{lem:fluctuation}
If $K$ has positive rates, every stationary state satisfies
$\mu([w]_B)\geq\kappa(K)^{|B|}$ for every finite $B$ and pattern
$w$. For any initial law $\nu$, output window $W$, lag $t$, and
$B$ containing the whole depth-$t$ backward dependency region,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:conditional}
 \Pr_\nu(X_t|_W=u\mid X_0|_B=w)=g_{K,t}(w;u)
\end{equation}
whenever the conditioning event has positive probability. The
function $g_{K,t}$ is independent of the exterior distribution.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Stationarity and conditionally independent site updates give
\[
 \mu([w]_B)=
 \int\prod_{z\in B}K(w_z\mid x|_{z+N})\,d\mu(x)
 \geq\kappa(K)^{|B|}.
\]
In the graphical construction, the output on $W$ at time $t$
is a fixed function of $X_0|_B$ and finitely many future uniforms,
independent of the initial state. Conditioning on the whole
pattern $w$ fixes every relevant initial input. Every global
extension therefore has the same conditional output law.
A sufficient region for radius $r$ is $B=W+[-rt,rt]^D$.
Conditioning on only part of it is insufficient in general.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Reweighting the fluctuations}
\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:transfer}]
Express the source's finitely many cylinder functions as finite
linear combinations of pattern indicators on one common output
window $W_{\mathbf k,t}$. Choose its whole backward region $B$.
For each output pattern $u$ and stationary $\mu$, set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reweight}
 q_u(\mu)=
 \sum_{w\in\Sigma^B}\lambda([w]_B)
 \frac{\Pr_\mu(X_0|_B=w,\ X_t|_W=u)}{\mu([w]_B)}.
\end{equation}
All denominators are positive by Lemma~\ref{lem:fluctuation}.
Its conditional identity gives the exact finite equality
\begin{equation}\label{eq:transferexact}
 q_u(\mu)=\sum_w\lambda([w]_B)g_{K,t}(w;u)
                         =(\lambda P_K^t)([u]).
\end{equation}
Evaluate the source's $R_{\mathbf k,t}$ on these probabilities.
The transferred statistic equals the source statistic at every
finite tuple and lag, in every stationary state. Its iterated
limits exist whenever the source limits do, with the same order
and value.

The sum is finite, its weights are computable, and division is
computable on the positive-denominator domain without a supplied
uniform lower bound. The source domain pulls back to an effectively
open domain. Thus the new statistics are admissible finite
space-time functionals. Counting $t$ as one added innermost
index gives depth $d+1$. The transformation uses the specified
seed, family and geometry and no target oracle. It preserves
target independence where those inputs have it. Equality
\eqref{eq:transferexact} also proves the robustness assertion.
\end{proof}

The weights are essential. Waiting for an unspecified fluctuation
samples patterns with frequencies $\mu([w]_B)$, not
$\lambda([w]_B)$. One interpretation first samples a desired
pattern with the source's computable finite law, then waits for
it. Conditional on the past its chance at each next update is
at least $\kappa^{|B|}$; avoiding it for $n$ updates has probability
at most $(1-\kappa^{|B|})^n$. The local Markov identity applies
after that occurrence. This concerns repeated finite events,
not one positive-probability event containing an entire infinite
cloned beginning. No useful uniform sampling efficiency is claimed.

For the canonical cloned uniform row the weights have a simple
schema: a finite pattern has zero weight unless transverse sites
at each common line coordinate agree; otherwise its weight is
$|\Sigma|^{-j}$, for $j$ distinct specified line coordinates.
Alphabet and block metadata determine these numbers. They contain
neither $\beta$ nor its approximants. Finite cloned patterns
remain explicit in the readout, although cloning is not a required
initial condition of the eternal world.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Corollary~\ref{cor:sharp}]
Transfer each realization of Theorem~\ref{thm:realization},
including the tuple-indexed ones. This attains every $\D_{d+2}$
value at eternal depth $d+1$ in every stationary state. For the
upper bound, restrict all-state consensus to the nonempty
$\ST(K)$ at a computable kernel in the robust region and apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:ceilings}. At depth $d$ its ceiling is
$\D_{d+1}$, so a value outside that class cannot have such a
consensus realization. Over all admissible readouts, lower-level
values can also be attained by computable arrays of constants.
This does not say that one fixed gap formula attains every value
in one fixed automaton.
\end{proof}

The transfer is a use of the Markov property and positive rates.
For fixed $K$, its finite values can also be computed directly
from $\lambda,K$ and are independent of $\mu$. This breadth
comes from the admitted class of probability functionals.
The result is not a theorem that a bounded local observer
efficiently estimates these ratios, nor a minimal-cost theorem
for every source law. The arithmetic obstruction is sharp for
values outside the lower consensus ceiling.
