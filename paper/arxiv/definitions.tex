\section{Definitions and scope}\label{sec:definitions}
We compare two ways of assigning a number to an infinite noisy system.
A begun law takes a time limit from prescribed initial data, followed by
scale limits. An eternal law takes scale limits of stationary ensemble
statistics. Stationarity supplies no extra time limit. At equal scale
depth this distinction changes an arithmetic ceiling by one level.
The comparison concerns expectation functionals and effective consensus
classes. It makes no assertion that a finite experiment completes a limit.

\subsection{Dynamics and preparations}
Let $\Sigma$ be a finite labelled alphabet, $N\Subset\Z^D$ a finite
neighbourhood, and $K(a\mid r)$ a stochastic vector for every
$r\in\Sigma^N$. The probabilistic cellular automaton (PCA) $P_K$
updates sites independently conditional on the previous configuration.
It commutes with translations. Positive rates mean
\[
 \kappa(K)=\min_{a,r}K(a\mid r)>0.
\]
The finite product of row simplices has its relative topology. Open
kernel sets below are relatively open in this space. Computability
means computability of all finitely many entries. The ceiling results
also apply to kernels with zero entries; the realization and
fluctuation results explicitly require positive rates.

Write $\M$ for probability measures on $\Sigma^{\Z^D}$ with the weak
topology and $\M_{\rm TI}$ for the translation-invariant ones. The
stationary TI class is
\[
 \ST(K)=\{\mu\in\M_{\rm TI}:\mu P_K=\mu\}.
\]
A phase means an extreme point of this compact convex class. This
definition does not identify extremality with shift ergodicity.
An eternal world is a stationary ensemble, with its stationary path
law when histories are observed. A begun world has law
$\lambda P_K^t$ at age $t$, where $\lambda$ is a specified computable
TI measure unless another hypothesis is stated.

For the constructions, coordinates are $(a,b,i)\in\Z^2\times\Z$.
The noiseless line rule uses an enlarged pre-blocking alphabet $A$.
Grouping $B$ consecutive cells gives the physical alphabet
$\Sigma=A^B$, as specified in Section~\ref{sec:renewal}.
An admissible row law is the blocked image of a full-support,
one-cell-shift-invariant ergodic law on $A^\Z$. This universality
class concerns laws before blocking; it does not include arbitrary
shift-ergodic laws on $\Sigma^\Z$.
A cloned row has $x(a,b,i)=y(i)$. Its law is TI in all three
coordinates. Our canonical seed clones a uniform Bernoulli row on
$\Sigma$; it is the blocked image of the uniform Bernoulli row on
$A$ and is therefore included. There is no distinguished site or
finite program in this computable seed. There is a time-zero
preparation and exact infinite transverse redundancy. This seed is
not a product measure on $\Z^3$.

\subsection{Readouts and the counting of limits}
\begin{definition}[Effective finite statistics]\label{def:statistics}
A scale-depth-$d$ family has indices $\mathbf k\in\N^d$ and
\[
 s_{\mathbf k}(\mu)=
 R_{\mathbf k}\bigl(\mu(F_{\mathbf k,1}),\ldots,
                         \mu(F_{\mathbf k,j_{\mathbf k}})\bigr).
\]
One algorithm, uniformly in every index, returns all finite supports,
rational cylinder functions $F$, and a real-computable evaluator
$R$ on an effectively open domain. It computes the value to any
requested rational accuracy wherever defined. A stationary-history
statistic may use finitely many space-time cylinders instead.
The family is bounded linear if $s_{\mathbf k}(\mu)=\mu(F_{\mathbf k})$
with $|F_{\mathbf k}|\leq1$.
\end{definition}
The eternal law and begun law are respectively
\begin{align}
 \Phi(\mu)&=\lim_{k_1}\cdots\lim_{k_d}s_{\mathbf k}(\mu),
       \label{eq:eternal}\\
 \Phi_{\rm beg}(\lambda,K)&=
   \lim_{k_1}\cdots\lim_{k_d}\lim_{t\to\infty}
          s_{\mathbf k,t}(\lambda P_K^t).
       \label{eq:begun}
\end{align}
All limits are finite, and every intermediate limit must exist for
each fixed tuple of remaining indices. These are partial functionals;
their admissible indices need not be decidable. At $d=0$, the eternal
law has no limit, while the begun law still has its time limit.
Ratios are used only on a positive-denominator domain. A clock-free
begun family has $s_{\mathbf k,t}=s_{\mathbf k}$: its statistic uses
scale but not age. A lag in a stationary history is an ordinary index;
if sent to infinity it counts toward depth.

Consensus on $C\subseteq\ST(K)$ means the entire law is defined
and has the same value on every member of $C$. Robust begun
realization means one value for all $K$ in a nonempty open set $U$.
Robust all-state eternal consensus means one value for every
stationary state at every $K\in U$. A selected state is not a
consensus class. All our origin-free statements mean that translating
the finite reference window changes no ensemble value; they do not
remove the window's coordinate or block presentation.

\subsection{Arithmetic and effective classes of states}
Let $0^{(n)}$ denote the $n$th Turing jump, with $0^{(0)}$ computable.
Put
\[
 \D_n=\{x\in\R:x\text{ has a fast Cauchy name computable in }
                        0^{(n-1)}\},\qquad n\geq1.
\]
Thus $\D_1$ is the computable reals and $\D_2$ the limits of
computable rational sequences. The finite jump hierarchy is strict.
One existing limit of a uniformly $X$-computable real sequence is
computable in $X'$.

We fix a compact representation compatible with cylinder Cauchy
names. For each probability $p$, code
$2p-1=\sum_{n\geq1}d_n2^{-n}$ with $d_n\in\{-1,0,1\}$,
and interleave all these signed digits. Three symbols are encoded
by two bits, excluding the fourth pair by an effective closed
condition. Decoding is computable and continuous. Conversely,
given Cauchy data for a residual $y\in[-1,1]$, choose $d=-1,0,1$
by certifying, respectively, $y<0$, $|y|<1/2$, or $y>0$;
these overlapping alternatives cover the interval. Replace $y$
by $2y-d$ and continue. This uniformly computes a code without
testing equality to a dyadic rational. Normalization and marginal
consistency impose further effective closed conditions on the
coordinate arrays. A state is low when it has such a name $X$
with $X'\equiv_T0'$.
An effective closed class means a $\Pi^0_1$ class of these valid
codes; an effective $\Sigma^0_2$ class is a computable union of
such closed pieces. This is stronger than an arbitrary noneffective
$F_\sigma$ description. All state-class hypotheses use this fixed
representation, including validity constraints.

Uniform finite statistics are crucial: a noncomputable site map
would be advice. Even a computable decoder can carry a target
program when it varies with the target. The upper bounds quantify
over all effective readouts. For attainment we instead fix a uniform
readout schema, independent of the target, parameterized only by
the finite labelled alphabet, block length and the prescribed depth.
The target program goes into the finite dynamics. We do not claim
one literal observation table on one common alphabet for all targets.
