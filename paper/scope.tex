\section{Inquiry, prior work and remaining questions}\label{sec:scope}
\subsection{Unlearnability is a fixed-target statement}\label{subsec:reach}
An effective inquiry has finitely encoded histories, a computable
adaptive controller choosing each finite observation and rational
estimate, and uniformly computable finite conditional outcome
probabilities on positive-probability histories. Each successful
stage finishes in finite time; no computable cost or convergence
bound is required. The target is fixed independently of the outcome
tape. Auxiliary finite experiments, including Born-random outcomes,
are admitted only with the same effective conditional description.

For computable $K$ and the canonical computable seed $\lambda$,
adaptively chosen finite space-time cylinders of the begun history
satisfy these hypotheses. Finite-cone sums compute every finite
joint observation probability uniformly. On a positive-probability
history, division by its computable positive probability computes
the next conditional law. The controller chooses finite windows,
observation times and outputs computably. Thus these observations
meet the hypotheses of the released fixed-target randomized reach
theorem \cite[Theorem~21.1]{criticalcurrent}. It follows that
convergence to one prescribed real with positive probability
forces that real into $\D_2$. Conversely a program can print a
chosen computable rational approximation to any $\D_2$ target
while ignoring observations. This existential converse is not
one common learner for an unknown system.

The companion proof combines effective sampling, almost-everywhere
generalized lowness and a relativized positive-measure cone
argument. Its conclusion concerns a fixed target, not every
realized random limit. Accordingly, our values outside $\D_2$
cannot be learned with positive probability by these inquiries at
computable begun data. Noncomputable kernels or preparations can
supply advice; no such claim is made for arbitrary noncomputable
parameters or arbitrarily selected noncomputable stationary laws.
The realization, consensus and transfer proofs do not depend on
the companion's inquiry theorem; only this inquiry conclusion
uses it.

The anchored precursor \cite[Theorem~1.5]{criticalcurrent} uses
one fixed reliable rule and a distinguished program origin.
Here the finite program moves into the rule, and self-organization
removes that origin in the seed. Both cited theorems are in the
public version 2.1.0 of \emph{Uncomputable critical exponents},
with version DOI \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.23780285}.

\subsection{Prior mechanisms and the scope of the synthesis}
Noiseless realization of limit-computable target measures from
full-support one-cell-shift-ergodic $A$-row laws is due to Hellouin de Menibus
and Sablik \cite{hdms}. Boyer--Delacourt--Poupet--Sablik--Theyssier
\cite{boyer} obtain high arithmetic complexity for persistent-word
languages of noiseless $\mu$-limit sets. These are substantive
antecedents of the begun construction. Our use is at fixed
positive noise on an open kernel region, for a uniform real-valued
readout schema.

Positive-rate storage and reliable computation come from Toom,
G\'acs--Reif and G\'acs \cite{toom,gacsreif,gacstoom,gacsself}.
The present paper does not introduce a new reliability paradigm.
The conditional certificate argument composes their local-tree
bounds with renewal denominators and continuous quantization.
G\'acs's self-organizing construction uses manufactured
synchronization and treats memory and computation initialization
separately \cite{gacsself}; those features are evidence for a route,
not proof that every possible construction must contain a clock.

The eternal ceiling uses low basis, compact consensus and
iterated limits, with Choquet representation for the bounded
linear extension. The unique-stationary-measure result is already
Marcovici--Sablik--Taati \cite{marcovici}. Taati \cite{taatinoise}
proves rapid forgetting for surjective CA with positive additive
noise. Such results concern particular dynamics and noise models;
they do not exclude the reliable stacks used here.
Marsan--Sablik \cite{marsan} study invariant measures in the
zero-noise limit, including effective constraints and realization
of connected compact limit sets. Their noise parameter tends to
zero, whereas ours stays in a fixed positive-rate open region.
The history/Gibbs correspondence is prior work
\cite{domany,goldstein,miekisztaati}.

The bounded primary-source comparisons available to us found no
earlier statement of the full robust renewal realization with its
seed quantifiers, nor of its all-state fluctuation-transfer and
effective-consensus comparison. This is a bounded-search outcome,
not an exhaustive priority claim. Most of the mechanisms above
are classical; the new statements are the specified compositions
and quantifiers.

\subsection{What is required, and what is still open}
At fixed depth, effective stationary consensus is the hypothesis
that imposes the ceiling. A selected eternal state may carry a
non-low value. Our beginning supplies one $\Pi^0_2$ stationary
cluster-class specification, but another noneffective selection
could supply a similar class. There is no theorem that a physical
or cosmological beginning is its only possible description.

The world's age is absent from the clock-free cluster readout and,
after quantization, from the strict begun statistic. Infinite
cloned redundancy remains in those preparations. Eternal transfer
needs no cloned initial state, but uses finite cloned-pattern
weights and an elapsed-time lag; the static form uses a reference
direction and slab separation. These resources have explicit
places in the definitions. Neither the eternal observer nor a
finite simulation completes any of the limits.

\begin{conjecture}[Product-seed realization]
A TI positive-rate PCA admits a robust begun law outside $\D_2$
under a target-independent effective readout schema from a full
product seed, with a suitably specified seed-universality class.
\end{conjecture}
Removing the two cloned directions requires a new construction.
A possible route is a self-organized synchronization layer carrying
the noiseless segment computation as payload. A self-built clock
may help; its universal necessity is not proved. The direct
comparison obstruction in Proposition~\ref{prop:basin} does not
settle this problem.

\begin{conjecture}[Individual transfer cost and restricted observations]
Characterize the least eternal depth of a given begun law when
the physical observation schema is fixed, and determine when
the sufficient additional limit of Theorem~\ref{thm:transfer}
is necessary.
\end{conjecture}
Constants already show that it is not necessary for every law.
The arithmetic ceiling proves necessity for values outside the
lower consensus class, but does not classify all individual
readouts. A further question is a quantum version with precisely
specified effective local channels, stationary-state classes and
conditional observation operations. The classical finite-cone
identity cannot simply be replaced by conditioning quantum
states without defining the measurement and disturbance model.

\section*{How this work was done}
The authors developed the definitions and proofs, checked the
specified primary statements, and take responsibility for the
mathematics and attribution. The substantial imported
self-organization and reliability constructions are identified
where used; a complete local table for those large automata is
not generated or exhaustively enumerated here.

The ancillary programs check exact renewal-cylinder identities,
block-coordinate formulas, finite cones, conditional transfer,
certificate constants and continuous-quantizer plateaus.
Independent implementations and explicit counterexamples test
the limitations of unconditional independence, modal decoding
and guarded updates. Seeded finite-torus simulations illustrate
preparation dependence, metastability and failure of particular
guarded designs. Observations are kept separate from mathematical
pass conditions; no preregistration claim is made from final files.
All intended numerical criteria raise on failure.

In particular, high sitewise agreement in a guarded rule-30
experiment can come from a spatially alternating fixed row rather
than active computation. Clean-clone tests expose the same design's
large tracking errors. These are finite-size diagnostics, not
universal obstructions. The chosen simulation noise values are
not certified members of the tiny analytic reliability region.
Every finite positive-rate torus has a unique stationary law;
its observed branches are finite-time metastability, not distinct
stationary phases. Exact infinite-volume claims, noncomputability,
seed universality and every-scale error bounds rest on the proofs.
The sequential runner records sampled process-tree RSS, not an
operating-system historical peak. Per-script memory fields may
be absent where a platform API is unavailable.
