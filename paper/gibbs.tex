\section{Stationary histories and static Gibbs states}\label{sec:gibbs}
For a positive-rate kernel, put one translated energy
\[
 \varphi_K(x)=-\log K\bigl(x_{0,0}\mid
                              x|_{N\times\{-1\}}\bigr)
\]
at each point of $\Z^D\times\Z$. Each interaction has finite
range and bounded finite energy at fixed $K$. Row normalizations
are retained. At computable $K$ the interaction is computable.

Extend a stationary PCA state $\mu$ to its consistent bi-infinite
path law $\Gamma_\mu$. Changing finitely many space-time spins
changes exactly the transition factors whose parent-child families
meet that set. Conditional probability ratios are products of
these $K$ factors and hence exponentials of the corresponding
energy differences. This is the finite-range DLR specification.
Factors centred at future children of resampled spins are included.

The converse for fully space-time TI Gibbs states is the
PCA/Gibbs correspondence of Goldstein--Kuik--Lebowitz--Maes
\cite{goldstein}, restated by Taati--Mi\k{e}kisz
\cite[Theorem~3.2]{miekisztaati}. Such a Gibbs state has the PCA's
forward transition law and a stationary TI time-zero marginal.
Full space-time invariance is part of this converse. It is not
inferred for arbitrary non-TI Gibbs states, and a one-time
marginal is not itself the four-dimensional Gibbs history.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Corollary~\ref{cor:gibbs}]
Take a fully TI Gibbs state $\Gamma$ for $\varphi_K$.
By the correspondence it is a stationary PCA history. The
two-time probabilities in \eqref{eq:reweight} are static
cylinders on two slabs separated by $t$ in the fourth direction.
Equations~\eqref{eq:conditional} and \eqref{eq:transferexact}
hold. Apply the depth-two transfer of the depth-one realization.
Every such state has value $\beta$, with slab separation sent
to infinity as the added limit.

For a computable finite-range finite-alphabet interaction, full
TI Gibbs classes have effective closed code descriptions:
impose all local DLR equations against finite exterior cylinder
tests and all translation equations. Finite conditional weights
are computable and positive, so failed equations have finite
precision witnesses. Nonemptiness follows from finite-volume
Gibbs measures, compactness and translation averaging, as boundary
fractions vanish on growing boxes. The low-basis and compact-cover
arguments of Section~\ref{sec:consensus} give the same consensus
ceilings. A depth-one consensus at computable parameters cannot
equal a fixed value outside $\D_2$. A computable kernel witness
transfers the robust ceiling.
\end{proof}

The map $K\mapsto-\log K$ transports the open kernel region into
the normalized PCA interaction family, not into an open set of all
four-dimensional interactions. Uniform-fault weights can instead
be parameterized by inverse temperature $b$: the total wrong-symbol
rate is $(q-1)e^{-b}/(1+(q-1)e^{-b})$, for alphabet size $q$.
A sufficiently large open interval of $b$ stays in the reliable
region with normalization preserved.

A begun half-space formulation fixes a cloned bottom row, uses
the causal law above it, and then averages over the prescribed row
distribution. Interior DLR ratios have the same transition-factor
calculation, including future children. Its begun statistics are
those already proved. When full-space time invariance is needed
in a limiting history, use Ces\`aro time shifts or prove it
separately. Ordinary accumulation alone does not give invariance.
The static all-state result uses the correspondence and transfer,
not an assumption of a unique full-space Gibbs phase.
